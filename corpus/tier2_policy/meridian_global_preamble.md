This Manual applies to all employees of Meridian Global Services and its subsidiaries. Where a country chapter of this Manual addresses a matter, that chapter governs for employees of that country's entity. Where it is silent, this global preamble governs. Nothing in this Manual reduces any entitlement conferred by the applicable statutory law of the country of employment; where a provision of this Manual confers less than the statutory minimum, the statutory minimum applies.

This Manual may be amended by the Group People Committee. An amendment takes effect from the effective date stated in the amending instrument. Where an amendment states no effective date, it takes effect from the date of its publication to employees. Amendments are not applied to entitlements that have already crystallised before the amendment's effective date, save where the amending instrument expressly so provides.
