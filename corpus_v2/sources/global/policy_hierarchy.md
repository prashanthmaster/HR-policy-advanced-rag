# Meridian Global Services — policy authority and hierarchy

> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer

## 1. Governing order

An employee's entitlement is determined first by mandatory law applicable to
the employing entity and work location, then by an enforceable contract or
collective instrument, then by the current country policy, and finally by this
global handbook. A lower-level document may give a more beneficial entitlement
where law permits, but it cannot reduce a mandatory minimum.

The newest publication is not automatically controlling. A document must be
approved, applicable to the employee, and effective on the date relevant to the
question. A published future amendment remains pending until its effective date.
An expired or superseded document remains available only for historical as-of
questions.

## 2. Jurisdiction boundaries

India, UAE mainland, DIFC, and ADGM are distinct legal scopes. The phrase "UAE
employee" is insufficient when the employing entity or free-zone jurisdiction is
unknown. Global HR must obtain the employing entity before deciding notice,
leave, or end-of-service benefits.

## 3. Conflicts and uncertainty

Where two approved documents appear inconsistent, HR must not choose whichever
was retrieved first. The case is escalated with both source IDs, versions, and
effective dates. An unresolved conflict is reported as insufficient evidence.

## 4. No implied entitlement

An FAQ can explain an approved rule but cannot create or amend an entitlement.
Examples are illustrative and do not replace the governing formula. Silence in
one document does not prove that no entitlement exists elsewhere.
