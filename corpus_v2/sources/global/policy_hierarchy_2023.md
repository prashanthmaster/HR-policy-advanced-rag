Mandatory local law prevailed over the global handbook. A country addendum applied
after the global handbook where it provided more specific terms. The historical
document did not clearly distinguish publication date from effective date and did
not define how unresolved conflicts were escalated.

contract or collective instrument, country policy, and global handbook. This
