## Required case facts

Before HR gives a personalised gratuity, notice, or leave answer, the case record
must identify the employing entity, work jurisdiction, employee category,
contract type, service dates, relevant event date, and the exact question being
asked. Monetary calculations also require the correct wage basis and currency.

The system may ask a targeted clarification. It must not infer that every UAE
employee works in the mainland, that every Indian employee is a worker covered by
the same statutory provision, or that total compensation equals statutory wages.

## Date meanings

Publication date records when a document became available. Effective date records
when its rule starts. Revision date records an editorial change. These dates are
not interchangeable. A retroactive amendment may be published after its effective
date; that relationship must remain explicit.

## Evidence records

Every material answer must preserve the source ID, document version, clause ID,
locator, corpus generation, and exact supporting excerpt. If extraction loses the
locator or the document hash differs from the approved manifest, the source is not
eligible for publication.

## Privacy

Evaluation cases use synthetic people and amounts. Real employee names, medical
records, bank details, government identifiers, and disciplinary records must not
be placed in the portfolio corpus or evaluation logs.
