# India annual leave — reviewed statutory scope note

This project-authored extract is derived from the Occupational Safety, Health and
Working Conditions Code, 2020 and official implementation material. It is not legal
advice and does not claim universal coverage for every Indian employee.

## Certified demo rule

The Code contains an annual-leave-with-wages framework for workers within its
applicable establishments. Eligibility and accrual cannot be answered safely from a
country name alone. The system must first establish whether the person and
establishment fall within the relevant statutory provision and must distinguish a
statutory minimum from a more beneficial company entitlement.

For the active portfolio cases, the statutory calculation fixture uses one day of
leave for every twenty days worked after the applicable qualifying threshold is met.
The RAG system must not turn that fixture into a universal statement covering every
employee, industry, state rule, or leave category.

If establishment type, worker status, or relevant working-day facts are missing, the
correct response is a clarification request rather than a confident number.
