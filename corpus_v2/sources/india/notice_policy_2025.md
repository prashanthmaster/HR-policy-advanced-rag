# Meridian India — notice policy, 2025 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version is effective from 1 July 2025 and supersedes the 2023 notice policy.

Confirmed employees in grades A1 through A3 must give thirty calendar days'
written notice when resigning. Confirmed employees in grades M1 and M2 must give
forty-five calendar days. Employees in grades M3 and above must give sixty
calendar days. Meridian applies the corresponding period when ending employment
without misconduct, subject to mandatory law, contract terms, and the nature of
separation.

The employee's grade on the date notice is submitted determines the company
period. A promotion after notice is submitted does not retrospectively extend it.
An employee on probation is outside this table and requires a separate decision.

Any waiver or payment in lieu must be recorded in writing. HR must state whether
the quoted period comes from company policy, contract, or mandatory law.
