## Certified statements

For this corpus, 21 November 2025 is the transition date on which the four central
Labour Codes were brought into force. Questions with an as-of date before that date
must not automatically be answered from a post-commencement rule. Questions on or
after that date must not rely on a repealed central enactment merely because its text
is semantically similar.

The four Codes are the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the
Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working
Conditions Code, 2020. Commencement does not eliminate applicability questions. The
employee category, establishment type, jurisdiction, contract, and any applicable
rules or notifications can still affect the answer.

This note certifies only the transition date and source lineage needed by the demo.
It does not certify every provision of every Code.
