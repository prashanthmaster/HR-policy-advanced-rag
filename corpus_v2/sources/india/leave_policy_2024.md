# Meridian India — leave policy, 2024 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version is effective from 1 July 2024 and supersedes the 2023 leave policy.

Confirmed employees accrue twenty-four working days of privilege leave per full
year, credited at two days for each completed month of eligible service. Leave
earned before 1 July 2024 remains calculated under the historical rate. A query
covering both periods therefore requires a split-period calculation.

The carry-forward ceiling remains forty-five working days. An excess balance is
not automatically paid unless an approved rule or separation provision requires
payment. Casual and sick leave remain separate and are not encashable under this
policy.

Company privilege leave must be distinguished from any statutory annual-leave
minimum applicable to the employee. Where the statutory result is more beneficial,
HR applies the mandatory minimum and records the source of the adjustment.
