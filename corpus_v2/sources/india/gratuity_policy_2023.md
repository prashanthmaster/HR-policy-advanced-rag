## Historical applicability

This version applied from 1 January 2023 until, but not including, 21 November
2025. It is retained for historical as-of questions and must not govern a later
entitlement.

Meridian paid gratuity according to the central legislation then applicable to
the employee. The ordinary company eligibility rule was five years of continuous
service, with the statutory exceptions for death or disablement preserved. The
company did not provide a separate top-up.

The calculation record used last-drawn basic salary plus dearness allowance and
recorded completed years plus a part-year exceeding six months in accordance with
the governing rule. The applicable statutory ceiling was determined on the date
the benefit became payable and was never stored as a permanent number in this
company policy.

This historical document must not be used to decide the post-21-November-2025 wage
definition or fixed-term employment treatment.
