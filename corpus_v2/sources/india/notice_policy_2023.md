# Meridian India — notice policy, historical 2023 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version applied from 1 January 2023 until, but not including, 1 July 2025.

Confirmed employees in grades A1 through A3 were required to give thirty calendar
days' written notice when resigning. Confirmed employees in grades M1 and above
were required to give forty-five calendar days. Meridian applied the same period
when ending employment without misconduct, subject to mandatory law and the
employment contract.

Employees on probation were governed by their appointment letter and applicable
law. HR was required to determine employee classification and could not apply the
confirmed-employee table automatically.

Waiver, garden leave, payment in lieu, and recovery of an unserved employee notice
required a written HR decision. Nothing in this policy authorised withholding an
undisputed statutory payment.
