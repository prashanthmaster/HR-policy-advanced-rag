# Meridian India — historical payroll gratuity procedure

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This procedure applied from 1 January 2023 until, but not including, 1 April 2026.

Payroll captured service start and end dates, termination reason, last-drawn basic
salary and dearness allowance, completed service, and the statutory ceiling then in
force. Manual worksheets required a reviewer signature before payment.

This procedure predates the post-21-November-2025 wage-definition workflow. It must
not be used for a current calculation merely because its arithmetic terms resemble
the current rule. The 2026 procedure supersedes it and adds contract type, corpus
evidence IDs, formula versions, and explicit missing-fact handling.
