## Does four years of service always qualify for gratuity?

No. The ordinary rule in the approved statutory material remains five years.
Exceptions, including the treatment of qualifying fixed-term employment, must be
assessed from the current law and employee facts. The FAQ cannot amend the law.

## Which salary figure is used for gratuity?

Do not use gross salary or total cost to company by default. Payroll must identify
the wage components included by the governing post-commencement definition and the
last-drawn amount relevant to the event date.

## How much resignation notice must I give?

State your grade, confirmation or probation status, appointment-letter period,
submission date, and type of separation. The current company table may then be
compared with mandatory requirements.

## How is leave handled across the July 2024 amendment?

Service before and after 1 July 2024 is calculated under different accrual rates.
The two results are combined without rewriting the historical balance.
