Meridian Global Services India pays gratuity in accordance with the governing
Indian statute applicable on the date the entitlement becomes payable. The
Company does not apply a lower eligibility threshold, lower calculation rate,
or narrower exception than the statutory rule. If an employment contract or
binding award provides a more beneficial gratuity term, that better term is
preserved. This policy creates no separate company gratuity top-up.

Before stating eligibility or calculating an amount, the case must identify the
employment type, service start and end dates, reason employment ended, and the
last-drawn wage components required by the governing statute. If a required
fact is missing, the employee must be asked for that fact rather than receiving
an assumed result.
