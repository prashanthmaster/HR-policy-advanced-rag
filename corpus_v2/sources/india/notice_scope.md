# India employment notice — reviewed statutory scope note

This project-authored canonical note records a deliberate limitation: India does not
have one universal resignation-notice number that can be returned for every worker
and establishment.

## Certified demo behaviour

Notice can depend on the type of separation, employee or worker classification,
applicable standing orders, contract terms, establishment coverage, and misconduct
or other statutory exceptions. A retrenchment rule must not be presented as the
ordinary resignation notice for all employees.

For Meridian's synthetic employees, the company policy and employment facts supply
the contractual resignation notice. The statutory layer acts as a constraint and a
source of applicability questions; it does not invent a single national contractual
notice period.

When the query omits grade, employment category, separation type, or as-of date, the
system must ask for the missing facts. This limitation is an intended quality feature,
not a retrieval failure.
