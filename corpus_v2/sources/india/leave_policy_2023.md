# Meridian India — leave policy, historical 2023 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version applied from 1 January 2023 until, but not including, 1 July 2024.

Confirmed employees accrued eighteen working days of privilege leave for each
complete year, credited proportionately each month. Up to forty-five unused days
could be carried into the next leave year. Casual and sick leave were separate
balances and could not be converted into privilege leave.

On separation, eligible unused privilege leave was processed under the governing
policy and mandatory law. The payroll record had to identify the balance date,
eligible days, wage basis, and any statutory constraint. This document did not
authorise encashment of casual or sick leave.

Employees who were not yet confirmed, workers covered by a different statutory
rule, and employees on extended unpaid absence required an applicability review.
