# Meridian UAE mainland — payroll end-of-service procedure

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**

## Intake

Payroll requires the employing entity, jurisdiction, nationality or benefit
eligibility, contract and working pattern, service dates, termination reason, last
basic monthly wage, and AED currency confirmation. Missing information is returned
as a clarification request.

## Calculation record

The deterministic engine divides monthly basic wage by thirty for the daily rate in
the approved portfolio formula. It applies twenty-one days per year to the first five
years and thirty days per year thereafter, handles eligible fractions according to
the governing rule, and checks the statutory cap. It then calculates any grandfathered
company supplement as a separate component.

Every result stores inputs, intermediate values, rounding, formula version, policy
version, and evidence IDs. The language model may explain the record but cannot
perform replacement arithmetic.

## Exceptions

DIFC, ADGM, UAE-national pension cases, disputed service, non-full-time patterns,
and missing statutory facts are routed for review rather than forced through the
mainland full-time formula.
