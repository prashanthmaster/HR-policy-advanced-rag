# Meridian UAE mainland — annual leave policy, historical 2022 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version applied from 2 February 2022 until, but not including, 1 June 2025.

Eligible employees with at least one year of service received thirty calendar days
of annual leave per leave year. Employees with more than six months but less than one
year of service accrued two calendar days for each month, subject to the governing
law and payroll convention.

Up to ten calendar days could be carried forward with written manager approval.
Approved statutory rights were not reduced by a manager's failure to respond. Leave
balances were recorded in calendar days and could not be compared directly with a
working-day policy from another jurisdiction.

Termination settlement required the applicable law, current balance, service period,
and wage basis. This historical version must not govern leave accruing on or after
1 June 2025.
