## Does every UAE employee use this FAQ?

No. Confirm the employing entity and whether the employment is mainland, DIFC, or
ADGM. This FAQ covers only the Meridian mainland entity.

## Is end-of-service benefit calculated from total salary?

The approved statutory extract uses basic wage for the covered statutory benefit.
Do not substitute total remuneration. Grandfathered Meridian supplements are shown
separately.

## Is my notice always thirty days?

No. Grade, contract, probation status, who is terminating, and the employee's next
destination can matter. The confirmed-employee company table cannot answer every
probation case.

## Is annual leave measured in working days?

The mainland policy records this entitlement in calendar days. A working-day number
from another jurisdiction must not be merged with it.

## Can this FAQ override the policy?

No. It explains approved documents and creates no entitlement.
