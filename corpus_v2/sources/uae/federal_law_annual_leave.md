# UAE mainland annual leave — reviewed statutory extract

This project-authored English extract supports portfolio evaluation and must be read
with the official law and implementing decisions.

## Official source

- Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 regulating labour relations:
  https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541

## Certified demo rules

Article 29 provides thirty days of annual leave for each year of service. For service
longer than six months but shorter than one year, the statutory pattern represented
in the demo is two days of leave for each month. Employer scheduling, notice of leave
dates, carry-forward, payment, and termination settlement must be resolved using the
law, executive regulation, and applicable company policy rather than one isolated
sentence.

The system must not describe calendar-day entitlements as working days. It must also
avoid applying mainland rules to DIFC or ADGM employees. When the user does not state
the UAE jurisdiction, service duration, or whether the question concerns accrual,
carry-forward, scheduling, or encashment, clarification is required.

A more beneficial company policy may supplement the minimum, but it cannot be
silently treated as the statutory rule.
