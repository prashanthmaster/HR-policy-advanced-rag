# Meridian UAE mainland — handbook scope

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This handbook applies only to employees of Meridian Global Services FZ-LLC whose
employment is governed by the UAE mainland private-sector labour framework. It does
not apply to an employee of a DIFC or ADGM entity.

Before answering a UAE question, HR records the employing entity, work location,
free-zone status, nationality where relevant to the statutory benefit, contract
type, working pattern, service dates, and event date. The word "Dubai" does not by
itself establish DIFC or mainland jurisdiction.

Mandatory law prevails over this handbook. A company entitlement that is more
beneficial remains available if the law permits. The current approved country
policy prevails over a general global explanation. Historical documents may be
used only for their effective periods.

All monetary examples use AED. Basic wage and total remuneration are different
fields. An answer or calculation that lacks the required wage basis must request
clarification.
