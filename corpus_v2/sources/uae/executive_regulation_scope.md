# UAE labour executive regulation — reviewed scope note

This project-authored note records the relationship between the UAE mainland Labour
Relations Decree-Law and its Executive Regulation.

## Official sources

- Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 concerning the Executive Regulation:
  https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1547
- MOHRE-hosted English document:
  https://mohre.gov.ae/assets/download/2617404c/Cabinet%20Resolution%20_Executive%20Regulations%20Decree-Law%20No.%2033.pdf.aspx

## Certified demo behaviour

The Executive Regulation is subordinate legislation. It supplies implementation
detail and cannot be treated as an unrelated company document. When the Decree-Law
sets a general entitlement and the Resolution supplies a calculation or procedural
rule, evidence from both may be required.

At the Phase 2B review date, the official UAE servers exposed the source URLs but
blocked automated binary acquisition in this environment. The corpus therefore keeps
this reviewed scope note and records the missing raw artifact explicitly. It does not
substitute a commercial commentary site or claim that a local PDF was verified.

Any answer needing a Resolution provision not captured in an approved extract must
return insufficient evidence until the official artifact is acquired and reviewed.
