# Meridian UAE mainland — annual leave policy, 2025 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version is effective from 1 June 2025 and supersedes the 2022 policy.

Eligible employees with at least one year of service receive thirty calendar days
of annual leave per year. Employees with more than six months but less than one year
accrue two calendar days for each month, subject to the governing statutory rules.

The approved company carry-forward limit is fifteen calendar days. Leave earned
before 1 June 2025 retains its historical character; the amendment changes the
carry-forward ceiling prospectively and does not convert calendar days to working
days.

HR must distinguish entitlement, scheduling, carry-forward, and termination
settlement. A question asking only "How many leave days do I get?" requires service
duration and jurisdiction before a personalised result is given.

Where mandatory law produces a more beneficial outcome, the statutory result
prevails and the adjustment is recorded with its source.
