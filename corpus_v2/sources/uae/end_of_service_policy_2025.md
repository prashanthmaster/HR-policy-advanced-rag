# Meridian UAE mainland — end-of-service policy, 2025 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version is effective from 1 January 2025 and supersedes the 2022 policy.

Eligible employees receive the statutory end-of-service benefit under the
governing mainland law. Meridian provides no loyalty supplement to an employee
whose continuous service started on or after 1 January 2025.

An employee whose continuous service started before 1 January 2025 retains the
historical company supplement of seven days of basic wage for each completed year,
subject to the employee remaining within the grandfathered mainland population.
The statutory and company components are calculated and cited separately.

Required facts are jurisdiction, nationality or statutory eligibility, full-time or
other working pattern, service dates, termination context, and last basic wage. The
system must not use gross remuneration as basic wage or apply this policy to DIFC or
ADGM employment.
