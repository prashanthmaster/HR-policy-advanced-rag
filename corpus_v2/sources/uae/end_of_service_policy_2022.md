# Meridian UAE mainland — end-of-service policy, historical 2022 version

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

This version applied from 2 February 2022 until, but not including, 1 January 2025.

Eligible foreign full-time employees received the statutory end-of-service benefit
calculated on basic wage. Meridian also paid a company loyalty supplement after
three completed years of continuous service equal to seven days of basic wage for
each completed year.

The company supplement was separate from the statutory calculation and had to be
shown as a separate line. It did not alter statutory eligibility, statutory rates,
or the statutory cap. Employees outside mainland scope required a different policy.

The supplement was withdrawn prospectively by the 2025 policy. Employees whose
service began before 1 January 2025 retained the historical supplement under the
grandfathering terms stated in the successor document.
