# UAE mainland end-of-service benefit — reviewed statutory extract

This English project extract is limited to portfolio evaluation. The controlling
official text remains the UAE legislation source.

## Official source

- Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 regulating labour relations:
  https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541

## Certified demo rules

For an eligible foreign full-time worker in the mainland private sector, Article 51
uses basic wage as the calculation basis. The statutory pattern represented in the
demo is twenty-one days of basic wage for each of the first five years of service and
thirty days of basic wage for each subsequent year. A fraction of a year is handled
proportionately after the employee has completed at least one year of continuous
service. The total statutory benefit is capped at two years' wage.

The system must not apply this extract to a UAE national, a DIFC employee, an ADGM
employee, or a person outside the Decree-Law's scope. It must also distinguish the
statutory benefit from any synthetic Meridian supplement.

Required calculation facts include jurisdiction scope, nationality or statutory
eligibility, service dates, basic monthly wage, working pattern where relevant, and
termination context. Missing material facts require clarification.
