# UAE mainland notice and probation — reviewed statutory extract

This English project extract is limited to the active portfolio scope. It is not a
replacement for the official bilingual legislation.

## Official source

- Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 regulating labour relations:
  https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541

## Certified demo rules

Article 43 provides that the contractual notice period must be no less than thirty
days and no more than ninety days. The same contractual notice period applies to both
parties, subject to the legislation's rules and exceptions. A company document may
select a number inside that range for a covered employee, but retrieval must not
return a future-dated amendment before it becomes effective.

Probation has separate notice mechanics under Article 9. The maximum probation
period is six months. Employer termination during probation requires at least
fourteen days' written notice. Different worker-notice rules apply when leaving the
State or moving to another employer in the State.

The system must ask whether the employee is on probation, who is terminating, where
the employee will go next, and which UAE jurisdiction applies. Mainland, DIFC, and
ADGM rules must never be blended.
