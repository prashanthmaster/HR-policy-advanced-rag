# Meridian UAE mainland — notice policy

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Meridian Global Services is fictional. This document exists only for the HR
> RAG portfolio demonstration and must never be presented as an actual employer
> policy or as legal advice.

For a confirmed mainland employee, the contractual notice period is thirty calendar
days for grades A1 through M2 and sixty calendar days for grades M3 and above. The
same contractual period applies to either party, subject to mandatory law and a
lawful ground for termination without notice.

An employee on probation is outside this table. HR must determine who is ending the
relationship and whether the employee is leaving the UAE or moving to another UAE
employer before stating a probation notice requirement.

Notice begins when valid written notice is received. A waiver, payment in lieu, or
garden-leave direction must be documented. The current grade and contract are checked
on the notice date.

A published amendment with a future effective date does not change the current
period. No unpublished manager instruction can amend this policy.
