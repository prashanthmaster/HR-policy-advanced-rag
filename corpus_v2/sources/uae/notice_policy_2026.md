This version is effective from 1 August 2026 and supersedes the 2022 notice policy.

For a confirmed mainland employee, the contractual notice period is thirty calendar
days for grades A1 through M2, sixty calendar days for grade M3, and ninety calendar
days for grades M4 and above. The same contractual period applies to either party,
subject to mandatory law and a lawful ground for termination without notice.

The employee's grade on the date valid written notice is received determines the
company period. A future promotion does not retrospectively change it. Probation
cases remain outside this table and require the Article 9 facts described in the
approved statutory extract.

Waiver, payment in lieu, and garden leave require written HR approval. The system
must use the 2022 version for an as-of date before 1 August 2026.
